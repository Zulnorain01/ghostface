FAQ

Questions from the brave.

Do I need my own API key?

Yes — and it's free. GhostFace Studio has no servers and no key of its own. Grab a free key from Google AI Studio in about 30 seconds, paste it once in the Studio's key settings, and you're set. Google's free tier includes image generation — the exact amount varies by project and region, and you can see your own quota in AI Studio.

Is my API key safe?

It's stored only in your browser's localStorage and sent only to Google's API when you hit Generate. It's never uploaded to us — there is no "us", the whole app runs on this static page.

What photos work best?

Clear, front-facing portraits with good light. The AI keeps your face, likeness and pose recognizable and only transforms the atmosphere around you — blurry or tiny photos give it less to work with.

What do I get with Pro?

Watermark-free downloads at 2048px instead of 1024px. One payment of $4.99, unlocked with a license key — no subscription, no account.

Can I post the results on TikTok?

That's the whole point. Every generation comes with a one-tap "Copy TikTok caption" button with spooky captions and hashtag sets picked for the trend.
